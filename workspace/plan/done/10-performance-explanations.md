# 10. Performance explanations

**Goal:** Show why a clip is likely to travel and how it is performing.

**Scope:** Establish a simple scoring model using moment value/rarity, trim quality, and stream audience fit; expose concise reason labels in the UI.

**Depends on:** 03, 07, 09.

**Acceptance checks:** Changing a relevant input can change expected reach; player can see at least one understandable reason for a clip's performance; formulas are centralized and tunable.
