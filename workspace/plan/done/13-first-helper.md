# 13. First automation helper

**Goal:** Automate one repetitive task without taking away meaningful moment choices.

**Scope:** Add one unlockable helper, with a narrow behavior such as automatically catching routine moments based on a simple rule.

**Depends on:** 06, 11, 12.

**Acceptance checks:** Helper can be unlocked; its behavior is visible and predictable; rare or high-value moment selection remains under player control.
