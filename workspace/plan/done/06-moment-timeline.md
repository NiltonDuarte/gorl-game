# 06. Moment timeline generation

**Goal:** Surface ordinary stream activity and occasional clip-worthy moments.

**Scope:** Define a deterministic/testable generator or schedule for moment arrival, rarity, and category; render moments on a timeline.

**Depends on:** 02, 03, 05.

**Acceptance checks:** Timeline shows a mix of ordinary and notable moments; channel pace affects arrival; moments carry a stable ID and enough metadata for selection and scoring.
