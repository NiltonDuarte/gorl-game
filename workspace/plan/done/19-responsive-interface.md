# 19. Responsive interface pass

**Goal:** Keep the core loop usable on smaller screens.

**Scope:** Adjust layout, text, hit targets, and scrolling for narrow viewports and common touch use.

**Depends on:** 04, 05, 07, 08, 12.

**Acceptance checks:** Core actions remain discoverable and operable at phone-sized widths; important counts remain readable; no primary panel is inaccessible due to clipping/overlap.

**Implemented:** Narrow layouts use Live, Clip Desk, and Channel tabs, with stream switching and larger shop controls. The 390 × 844 preview confirmed all three panels fit the phone viewport.
